    Card checks

    How it plays. Every one of the 449 different cards in these decks has its rules text handled: a general parser covers the common patterns, and the rest have their own scripts. That includes the combos: Sanguine Bond, Vito, Dina or Enduring Tenacity with Exquisite Blood or Bloodthirsty Conqueror; Basalt Monolith with Rings of Brighthearth into Staff of Domination, Aetherflux Reservoir or Walking Ballista; Karn with Mycosynth Lattice; and death-trigger drains with sacrifice outlets. Effects that multiply each other stack the way they should: token and counter doublers, Fiery Emancipation and Gisela, Terror of the Peaks with Dragon tokens, extra combats with Kaalia. Each player is a heuristic AI. It sees the board, plays to win and holds up counterspells and protection, but it won't find every line a strong human would. Click any card to read it. Space plays and pauses, and the arrow keys step through the game. Card data comes from the open-source Forge project.
