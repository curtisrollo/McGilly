    How it plays. Each card's real rules text is read and the common effects are modelled: mana and colours, creatures and combat keywords, commander tax and 21 commander damage, removal, board wipes, counterspells, protection spells, card draw, ramp, tutors, reanimation, tokens, anthems, life drain, the Sanguine Bond + Exquisite Blood loop, and planeswalker abilities. Every player is a simple AI. Cards it can't model (most combo engines and copy effects) still get cast but do nothing. Click any card to read it. Space plays and pauses; the arrow keys step.
